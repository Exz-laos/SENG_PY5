## Answer Key & Explanations

**Chapter 4**
1. **(A) spans** - Relative clause verb modifying "history" (singular).
2. **(C) leaving** - Gerund following the preposition "by".
3. **(B) carpenter** - Parallelism/Context. Tobei Masataka was a shrine and temple carpenter.
4. **(A) branch** - Compound noun ("branch office").
5. **(C) which** - Relative pronoun modifying "Takashimaya Department Store".
6. **(D) president** - Contextual noun indicating the elected role.
7. **(B) overseas** - Adverb modifying "expanded".
8. **(A) receive** - Infinitive verb following "to".
9. **(C) construction** - Parallelism (planning -> design -> construction).
10. **(D) damping** - Compound noun ("motion-damping systems").
11. **(B) in** - Preposition matching "involved in".
12. **(A) of** - Phrasal verb "composed of".
13. **(C) Both** - Correlative conjunction pairing with "and".
14. **(B) exaggerated** - Passive voice verb.
15. **(C) such** - Prepositional phrase "such as".

**Chapter 5**
16. **(C) devoted** - Phrasal verb "be devoted to".
17. **(A) ingredients** - Contextual noun.
18. **(B) well** - Phrase "as well as".
19. **(A) major** - Adjective modifying "research theme".
20. **(D) including** - Preposition/Participle introducing examples.
21. **(C) cultivated** - Past participle modifying "technologies".
22. **(B) hands** - Contextual noun/phrase "in the hands of".
23. **(C) which** - Relative pronoun.
24. **(B) employed** - Present perfect verb.
25. **(C) available** - Adjective following "has been".
